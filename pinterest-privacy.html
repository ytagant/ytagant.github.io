Privacy Policy — Islamic Creator Resources Pin Poster

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Islamic Creator Resources Pin Poster is a private automation tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own product pins to the owner's own Pinterest boards.

Data we access

  • Pinterest: permission to read the owner's own boards and to create pins on the owner's own boards.

How data is used

The accessed data is used only to publish the owner's own pins. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Data storage and revocation

Access tokens are stored privately by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account settings, which immediately stops this tool from accessing the account.
