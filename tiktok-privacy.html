Privacy Policy — TikTok Automation

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Data we collect

TikTok Automation collects only the minimum data required to function:

  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the app. These are used solely to upload and publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account.
  • Basic account info (TikTok open ID and username) returned during authorization, used only to confirm which account the app is publishing to.

2. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data of any TikTok user other than the single account owner who authorized the app. We collect no names, emails, phone numbers, locations, or viewing behavior of third parties.

3. How data is used

Tokens are used exclusively to call TikTok's Content Posting API (upload and publish the owner's own videos, with the AI-generated content label applied). Basic account info is used only to verify the target account before publishing.

4. Data storage and sharing

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation infrastructure and are never shared with any third party. No data is sold or used for advertising.

5. Revocation

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok's authorized-apps settings. Upon revocation the stored tokens become invalid and the app cannot perform any further action.

6. Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact the account owner through their TikTok account @BilliChooha.
