Terms of Service — TikTok Automation

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. What this app does

TikTok Automation is a private tool built for a single TikTok account owner. It publishes original cartoon videos that the owner produces to the owner's own TikTok account (@BilliChooha) through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The app performs no actions on any other account.

2. Authorization

The app acts only after the TikTok account owner explicitly authorizes it through TikTok's OAuth consent screen. Authorization can be revoked at any time from TikTok's authorized-apps settings, which immediately stops all app activity.

3. Content

All videos published through the app are original, AI-generated cartoon content created by the account owner. Every published video is disclosed as AI-generated via TikTok's AI-generated content label.

4. Data

The app stores only the OAuth tokens needed to publish on the owner's behalf. No personal data of any third party is collected, stored, or shared.

5. Acceptable use

The app is used solely to publish the owner's own original content to the owner's own account, in compliance with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

6. Contact

For questions about this app, contact the account owner through their TikTok account @BilliChooha.
