Privacy Policy - YT Channel 2

YT Channel 2 is a YouTube upload automation tool used by its owner to upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channel.

Data we access

With the owner's authorization, the app accesses YouTube Data API scopes needed to upload videos and manage channel content. It does not sell personal data.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely by the owner and are used only to perform authorized uploads. We do not share tokens or channel data with third parties except Google/YouTube services required for upload.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the project owner via the GitHub repository ytagant/ytagant.github.io.
