Privacy Policy — Anime Automation

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Anime Automation is a private automation tool used only by its owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel (Anime Underworld) and to access his own Google Drive folders.

Data we access

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
  • Google Drive: permission to read and manage files in the owner's own Drive folders used by this automation.

How data is used

The accessed data is used only to run the owner's own uploads. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Data storage and revocation

Access tokens are stored privately by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately stops this tool from accessing the account.
